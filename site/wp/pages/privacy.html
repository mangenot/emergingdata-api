title: Privacy

This page says what personal data EmergingData collects, why, and what you can ask about it. The site is published by EmergingTextiles.com SARL, 60 rue François 1er, 75008 Paris, France, which is responsible for the processing.

What is collected

When you subscribe, the site keeps your name, e-mail address, company and the details of your subscription (plan, dates, invoices). Card payments are handled by Stripe; the card number never reaches the site. When you log in, the site sets the cookies WordPress needs to keep you logged in. The web server keeps its usual access logs (address, page, time) for security and for a limited time. The site sets no advertising cookies.

Why

These data are used to provide the subscription, to invoice it, to answer your messages and to keep the site secure. They are not sold or passed on, except to the providers that make the service work (hosting, payment, e-mail), each bound by its own commitments.

How long

Account and subscription data are kept while the subscription is active and for the time the law requires for invoices. Server logs are kept for a few months.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the data held about you, or object to a processing, by writing to contact@emergingdata.net. You can also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL.
