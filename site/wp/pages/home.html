What a report contains

The overview

The editor's reading of the month, from the latest events and data releases down to the structural fundamentals of the country.

Five sections

Public finances and the currency; exports, imports and tariffs; politics, social unrest and labour costs; logistics and shipping; energy. Each with its analysis, the month's updates, the figures with their direction, the charts and the risks ahead.

Two views

The data view on screen and the full report for reading and printing. The figures refresh every day from the database; the text is the monthly edition, written and signed by the editor.

Who it is for

Sourcing, procurement, supply-chain and risk teams that work with these countries and need their numbers in one place, read by someone who has followed them for years. The reports do not replace a country desk; they give the figures, their direction and the reading of the month, in the time it takes to go through one page.
